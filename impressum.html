02 privacy

The public pages of this site set no cookies, embed no analytics or tracking services, and store nothing about visitors.

The protected area (Vault) can only be used with a personal account. For anyone who signs in, the following is stored: username, time of sign-in, IP address, browser identification (user agent), and which documents were downloaded when. Failed sign-in attempts are logged too, because that is what makes abuse visible and slows automated attacks down.

These entries are deleted automatically after 90 days. Passwords are stored only as an argon2id hash and cannot be read back, including by me. The data sits on a privately operated server in Switzerland and is not passed on to third parties.

The contribution graph on the home page is loaded from a third party (github-contributions-api.jogruber.de), which means your IP address is sent to that service.

Information about stored data, and its deletion, can be requested at any time using the contact address above.
