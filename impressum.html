Privacy

The public pages of this site set no cookies, embed no analytics or tracking services, and store nothing about visitors.

The protected area (Vault) can only be used with a personal account. For anyone who signs in, the following is stored: username, time of sign-in, IP address, browser identification (User-Agent), and which documents were downloaded when. Failed sign-in attempts are logged as well. That is necessary to detect misuse and to slow down automated attacks.

These entries are deleted automatically after 90 days. Passwords are stored exclusively as an argon2id hash and are unreadable even to me. The data sits on a privately operated server in Switzerland and is not passed on to third parties.

The contributions graph on the home page is loaded from a third party (github-contributions-api.jogruber.de); the visitor's IP address is transmitted to that service in the process.

Information about stored data, and its deletion, can be requested at any time via the contact address above.
